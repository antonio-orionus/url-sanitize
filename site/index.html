Remove tracking from links

Paste a link or a whole message. Tracking parameters and redirect wrappers come out; everything else stays as it was.

Runs in your browser. Nothing you paste leaves this page.

Options

Clean text


        

        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    What it removes
    
        
    
          Links you copy carry extra parameters that tell the site, and often an ad network, where
          you came from and who shared the link. They do nothing for the page itself. url-sanitize
          removes them with the rules maintained by ClearURLs, Brave and Firefox, about 1,450 in
          all:
        
    
        
      
          
    • 
            Campaign tags on any site: utm_source,
            utm_medium, utm_campaign and the rest of utm_*.
          
      • 
          
    • 
            Ad click IDs: Facebook's fbclid, Google's
            gclid, gbraid and wbraid, Microsoft's
            msclkid, Mailchimp's mc_eid, HubSpot's _hsenc.
          
      • 
          
    • 
            Share IDs that tie a link to the person who shared it: YouTube and
            Spotify si, Instagram igsh, X s and
            t, TikTok _t, Reddit share_id.
          
      • 
          
    • 
            Shop tracking: Amazon's /ref=… path and
            pd_rd_*, eBay's _trksid, AliExpress's spm.
          
      • 
          
    • 
            Redirect wrappers: Google, Facebook and YouTube links that bounce
            through a tracking page are replaced by the page they point to.
          
      • 
        
    
        
    
          Affiliate tags such as Amazon's tag stay unless you tick the option above,
          since removing them takes money from whoever shared the link.
